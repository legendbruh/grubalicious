ℹ Our story

Food should not simply fill you up.

It should give you something to remember. Grubalicious began as a single fold-out counter run by three cooks who couldn't agree on a cuisine - so they stopped choosing and started combining. Every dish here still carries that argument: a Roman crust with a Punjabi filling, a Southern sear with a Goan spice rack.

We buy from the same three vendors every morning, cook in small batches, and close the kitchen the moment the last pan is clean rather than the moment the clock says so. Nothing here is built to be fast. It's built to be worth the wait.
